# Red team log

Builder-side red team (the author attacking its own work; **not** an independent audit). Every run attacks the skill: "A professional team follows this literally. Where does it produce a wrong decision or a weak project?" Record the attack, the verdict (confirmed / partly / not reproduced), the fix applied to the skill, and the residual risk. Never record an attack without applying or consciously rejecting a fix.
